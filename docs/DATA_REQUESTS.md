# Data needed for qualified mooring and docking decisions

Public weather and chart feeds cannot establish the capacities of a particular vessel, pier or tug. The following records are needed before a crew can rely on numerical recommendations operationally. Owners listed below are likely sources, not confirmed contacts. Supply shareable records only; do not commit restricted drawings or publications.

| Required data | Minimum usable format and provenance | Blocked capability | Likely holder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pier face, fitting/fender/camel layout and condition | Surveyed WGS84 origin, true bearing, berth-frame xyz, datum, IDs, inspection date, occupancy and excluded fittings; CAD/GeoJSON plus CSV | Line geometry, search, holding, contact | Port engineering / NAVFAC / pier operator |
| Pier fitting ratings | Horizontal and angle-dependent capacities, load combinations, foundation limits, condition reductions and inspection authority per fitting ID | Equilibrium capacity, combined bollard loads, approvals | Pier engineer / facility operator |
| Fender force-deflection and energy | Manufacturer curve, units, loading/unloading behavior, velocity/temperature factors, contact length/height, rated energy, inspection status | Contact energy, hull loads, docking velocity limits | Fender manufacturer / pier engineer |
| Ship fitting/chock geometry and SWL | Per class and hull configuration, ship-frame xyz, allowed lead sectors, rope size, paired chock, supporting structure ratings | Line placement, deck leads, fitting capacity | Ship design authority / ship force |
| Loading particulars | Approved loading-condition displacement, forward/aft draft, trim, freeboard, CG and wind profile; effective dates | Loads, depth/draft validity, line vertical geometry, stability | Ship stability/loading documentation owner |
| Rope and wire properties | Manufacturer/spec/batch ID, dry/wet minimum strength, load-elongation curve, age/inspection, condition, splices, part/reeving geometry, chock friction evidence | Tension and WLL, tending, snatch/rupture estimates | Supply authority / rope manufacturer / deck department |
| Actual line inventory | Available lengths, specs, counts, serviceability and installed rest length/pretension by line ID | Search feasibility and handling sequence | Ship deck department |
| Tug fleet and availability | Certified ahead/astern bollard pull, propulsion type, directional limits, towline ratings, contact restrictions, staffing/availability and weather limits | Allocation, continuous holding, maneuvering | Tug operator / harbor master |
| Vessel low-speed dynamics | Dimensional mass/inertia, added-mass matrix, damping/maneuvering derivatives, valid speed/depth domain, sign convention, test cases and uncertainties | Qualified berthing dynamics and contact | Naval architect / model basin / ship design authority |
| Propulsion and steering behavior | Engine/rudder/thruster response, limits, delays and verified maneuvering trials | Approach and abort simulation | Ship design authority / vessel operator |
| Bathymetry and dredge/survey status | Official chart/survey edition, horizontal/vertical datum, coverage, uncertainty, obstructions, latest dredge depth and clearance policy | UKC, approach corridor, grounding checks | NOAA hydrographic office / port surveyor / harbor master |
| Forecast representativeness and wind basis | Source issue/valid times, averaging/gust durations, reference height, model grid/depth, datum, station-to-berth comparison | Load normalization and forecast acceptance | NOAA/provider metadata plus local engineering review |
| Local sea state and shelter | Measured or reviewed directional wave transmission/reflection and harbor geometry; vessel response evidence | Qualified wave forcing/response at berth | Port/coastal engineer / measurement program |
| Wake and traffic | Timestamped vessel traffic, speeds, distances, hull class plus a validated wake model or local measurements | Wake loads, traffic-aware windows | VTS / harbor master / wake monitoring program |
| Line-handling procedure | Approved rules, crews/stations, equipment limits, snap-back zones, sequence dependencies and reviewed timing | Operational line sequence and briefing | Deck officer / pilot / port operations |
| Abort and replan criteria | Accountable authority, tug/traffic contingencies, forecast expiry, observed deviations, alternate berths and decision times | Crew-use release and contingency planning | Commanding officer / pilot / harbor master |

Tug wash remains outside tonight's model. Missing wake or dynamics inputs mean unavailable/unqualified, never zero. A synthetic training record must carry synthetic provenance and cannot qualify an asset. Each supplied dataset should include revision, source permission, units, coordinate/datum conventions, uncertainty, applicability and a responsible reviewer. Numeric changes invalidate affected saved recommendations and require fresh independent review.
