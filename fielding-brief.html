Pier Review

Parallel parking for warships, powered by physics

The operational challenge

Drafting a safe mooring and pier-work plan is complex. Bridge teams often rely on static sketches and rough estimates that cannot readily reflect changing wind, current, tide or mooring geometry.

Statement of objectives

Give bridge teams dynamic situational awareness to replace guesswork with calculated loads, compare configurations and prepare a shared, actionable plan for safer pier-work execution.

How we solve it

Interactive planning + automated physics

Pier Review helps bridge teams compare mooring options and review calculated loads to support safer pier-work planning.

  • NOAA data + NSTM 582 checks

    Use NOAA data where available to assess geometry and loads against NSTM 582 (Mooring and Towing) referenced limits.

  • Efficient tug placement

    Configure tug positions and assistance to support precise maneuvering.

  • Structured mooring sequences

    Draft line-handling sequences that support coordinated operational readiness.

  • Intuitive graphical editing

    Drag and drop the ship, then edit mooring connections to compare plan configurations.

  • Automatic safety sheets

    Generate comprehensive Mooring Evaluation Safety Sheets for review and briefing.

  • Mapbox visual geography

    Explore the berth and surrounding area in a rich, interactive geographic view.

Operational use case

Before arrival or pier work, select a berth, place the ship and review wind, current and tide. Compare tug and line configurations, then brief the selected sequence and safety sheet. Reassess the plan when conditions change.

Target audience

Primary: bridge teams, deck officers, harbor pilots and mooring crews.

Supporting: tug coordinators, port operations and fleet readiness planners.

Fielding requirements

Proposed deployment configuration

Aligned with platforms already being deployed to ships, submarines and autonomous vehicles.

Kubernetes platform

Domino, UDS or vanilla Kubernetes to host and manage the application services.

GPU-accelerated physics

Dedicated GPU hardware acceleration for real-time physics calculation pods.

Single sign-on

Keycloak or enterprise SSO for identity authentication and access control.
